Architectural Heritage

A Restored 18th Century Sanctuary
Crafted with Quiet Reverence

Located in the historic quarter of the Medina, Riad L'Ambre d'Or was conceived as a private refuge where the vibrant energy of the souks gives way to the gentle murmur of falling water and the fragrance of blossoming bitter orange trees.

Restored by master Moroccan maalems (artisans), every corner showcases traditional techniques: hand-chiselled zellige tiles from Fez, hand-carved cedarwood ceilings, and polished natural lime plaster (tadelakt). Here, understated luxury meets mindful hospitality.
